# Ancestor Worship

> [!quote] [Ancestor worship - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestor_worship)
> Ancestor worship is based on love and respect for one's ancestors. In some cultures, it is related to beliefs that the dead have a continued existence, and may possess the ability to influence the fortune of the living. Some groups venerate their direct, familial ancestors. Other religious groups, consider veneration of the dead to be idolatry and a sin.
